# Product

## What this is

A self-hosted platform that gives a solo founder four working teams, each
running as its own Hermes agent instance:

- **Growth** — sales and marketing
- **Technical** — product and engineering
- **Finance** — runway, burn, unit economics
- **Design** — product design (software interface or physical product) and
  brand identity

The founder talks to one Orchestrator. It routes work, keeps the teams in
sync, and surfaces the things that need a human decision.

## Who it is for

Technical solo founders and two-person teams who are pre-hire, and anyone
handling data they will not put into a third-party SaaS.

Not for: large teams with real headcount in these functions. They have humans
and the coordination problem is different.

## The pitch

> Your AI team, running on your infrastructure, with a playbook you can read
> and edit. Your company's knowledge stays yours.

## Positioning

The category is crowded. CoFounder.AI launched June 2026 with a large waitlist
and a polished voice-first product. Salesforce, Kore.ai, and AI Hive all ship
prebuilt agent catalogues. Being first is not available. Being different is.

Three differences that a customer can actually see and feel:

1. **Self-hosted, BYOK.** Company data never leaves the founder's machine or
   VPS. A closed SaaS cannot match this without rebuilding.

2. **Skills are readable, editable files.** The founder can open
   `outreach_draft/SKILL.md`, see exactly what the Growth team believes and
   how it decides, and change it. Competing products are black boxes. This is
   the strongest differentiator — lead with it.

3. **Portable.** Leaving the platform means taking a folder of markdown. No
   lock-in is itself a feature to the audience above.

### What we do not compete on

Onboarding polish, voice UX, consumer-grade design. Do not try. The audience
that wants those is not the audience that wants self-hosting.

## What this is not

- Not autonomous. Every external-facing action is founder-approved.
- Not a predictor. It diagnoses current state and projects scenarios with
  stated formulas. It does not forecast growth from thin data.
- Not an HR team. Hiring decisions stay human. Explicitly out of scope.
- Not a replacement for a lawyer or an accountant. Drafts and flags only.

## Honest risks

- **Data thinness.** Early startups have noisy, sparse data. Agents that
  analyse it will sometimes pattern-match on nothing. Mitigate by refusing to
  make claims below a stated data threshold, and saying so.
- **Setup friction.** Self-hosting is the differentiator and also the barrier.
  Onboarding must be near-zero-thought or the advantage is wasted.
- **Unverifiable value.** Founders cannot attribute outcomes to advice. Show
  the work — evidence, reasoning, receipts — so value is visible even when
  impact is not measurable.

## Success criteria for v1

Not revenue. Not users. One founder uses it for four consecutive weeks and the
`company_brain` at the end reflects a company the agents actually understand.

If that fails, nothing downstream matters.
